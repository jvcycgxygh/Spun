## 🎵 What Is Spun?

Spun is a music player that looks like a classic CD spinning in your computer. It's designed for Linux users who want a fun and visual way to enjoy their local music collection. Spun also connects with Cider, a popular Apple Music client, so you can control your streaming music too—all from one beautiful, circular interface.

Think of it as a retro CD player you might have had in the 90s, but now it's a software window on your desktop. You can watch the disc spin, browse your albums, and play your favorite songs with just a few clicks.

## ✨ Key Features

- **CD-Style Interface** – The main window looks like a physical CD, complete with a realistic spinning animation while music plays.
- **Local Music Playback** – Play MP3, FLAC, WAV, and other common audio files stored directly on your computer.
- **Cider Integration** – Control Apple Music through Cider without switching apps. Play, pause, skip, and see what's playing.
- **Album Art Display** – Spun automatically shows the album cover of the currently playing track right in the center of the CD.
- **Playlist Management** – Create and manage your own playlists to organize your music.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts** – Use your keyboard to control playback quickly (e.g., Space for play/pause, arrows for track skipping).
- **Lightweight & Fast** – Uses minimal system resources, so it runs smoothly even on older hardware.

## 🎮 Using Spun

Once Spun is open, you'll see the CD interface. Here's how to use it:

- **Playing Local Music**: Click the "Browse" or folder icon to select a folder containing your music. Spun will scan for audio files and display them in a list. Click any song to start playing.
- **Skipping Tracks**: Use the on-screen next/previous buttons, or your keyboard's media keys if you have them.
- **Adjusting Volume**: Use the scroll wheel on your mouse while hovering over the CD, or drag the volume slider at the bottom.
- **Cider Connection**: If you have Cider installed and running, Spun will automatically detect it. A small Apple Music icon will appear—click it to switch control between local files and your Apple Music library.

## 🎨 Customization

Make Spun your own:

- **CD Color**: Change the disc's color scheme through the settings menu (gear icon in the corner).
- **Spin Speed**: Adjust how fast the CD spins during playback (from a relaxing slow spin to a fast DJ-style spin).
- **Window Size**: Resize the window to make your CD bigger or smaller. It scales beautifully.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

Most issues have simple fixes. Try these before seeking help:

- **Spun won't start**: Make sure the executable has permission to run. Right-click the file, go to Properties > Permissions, and check "Allow executing file as program."
- **No sound**: Check your system's audio settings—ensure the correct output device is selected.
- **Cider not detected**: Ensure Cider is running and logged in. Restart both applications if needed.
- **CD spins but no music**: Verify your music files aren't corrupted. Try playing a different file to test.

Spun is built for Linux and should work on most modern distributions (Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian, Arch, etc.) as long as they have a graphical desktop environment.

**Can I use Spun without Cider?**
Absolutely! Cider integration is optional. Spun works perfectly as a standalone local music player.

**Does Spun require an internet connection?**
No, local playback works fully offline. Internet is only needed if you're using Cider to stream from Apple Music.
